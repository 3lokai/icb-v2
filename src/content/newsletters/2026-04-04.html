Roaster Spotlight

Just Joined ICB.

Ekata Coffee
Malabar, Kerala

Ekata Coffee has just joined ICB with 4 coffees listed. Based in Kerala's Malabar region and founded in 2016, they operate their own agroforestry network built on biodynamic and carbon-negative farming practices — with certifications from IMO, Demeter, NPOP, and SCA. The focus is on traceability and farmer welfare across an organic, ethically sourced Indian coffee supply chain.
