This is issue 14, and it starts with a coffee nobody can agree on. In one week on r/IndiaCoffee, one drinker said Blue Tokai's Nano X Excelsa tasted like a shoe. Another rested it for 30 days and found fermented tamul while it was hot and port wine once it cooled. In the same week, five Coorg estate lots, one of them an Excelsa, went on shelves in more than 400 stores across Greece and Cyprus.

The directory now lists 18 Excelsas, and 11 of them are fermented or experimental lots. Among arabicas, the share is closer to one in four. So an argument about what Excelsa tastes like is often an argument about process as much as species. Also inside: a cave-fermented Kerala Excelsa in the spotlight, a new Hyderabad roaster, and three new guides. If you have tried an Excelsa, the question at the end is for you.

Freshly Added

What's New on ICB.

One roaster joined this fortnight (more on Percup below), so the catalogue did most of the moving: 51 coffees added across 17 roasters. The Bean Co Roasters in Kolkata brought 20 of them, including a barrel-ferment run of rum, wine, whiskey and brandy barrel lots, a grape-fermented and a frozen-cherry-fermented coffee, plus two monsooned lots. Naivo added three Ratnagiri Estate lots and Blue Tokai added Terroir Culture from the same estate. Ratnagiri lots from Blue Tokai and Dancing Goat both drew reviews on r/IndiaCoffee this fortnight.

Coffees Added

Process first, again. Of the 51 additions, 15 are fermented or experimental lots: 9 logged as experimental, 5 anaerobic and 1 carbonic maceration. Five are washed. Another 19 do not list a process at all.

Three more Excelsas. Three Excelsas were listed this fortnight, taking the directory to 18, and one of them is in the spotlight below. Across the directory, Excelsa and liberica are mostly sold as process showcases. Arabica is not.

Terravagamon Excelsa Anoxic Naturals

Bermito Coffee Roasters · Kozhikode · Vagamon, Kerala

Excelsa Anoxic natural Medium roast

A Kerala Excelsa, fermented for 120 hours without oxygen inside a natural cave. Bermito says the aim is a clean, defined fruit profile that keeps Excelsa's structure. One of the 11 fermented lots among the directory's 18 Excelsas, and not yet rated.

Roaster Spotlight.

Percup Coffee Roasters

Hyderabad, Telangana · coffees being added

Percup joined ICB from Hyderabad this fortnight. It roasts in 1 kg batches on an Aillio Bullet R1 and works with limited-lot, traceable Indian single origins rather than commercial blends, including naturals from Anugraha Estate, a woman-led organic farm in Kodaikanal. Its sourcing is built around ethical and regenerative farming. The coffees are still being added to the directory, so the profile is the place to start.

Funky processing, and the notes that don't show up

A drinker who keeps returning to Indian coffee made a careful argument. They have no problem with estates chasing fermentation, since most coffee here is grown for yield and disease resistance, and processing is how it builds character. The complaint is narrower: the cup rarely delivers what the bag promises. Their latest example was a Wild Ferment lot from Home Blend, but the thread is about how tasting notes get written, not about one roaster.
