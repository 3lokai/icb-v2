You'll have noticed we skipped a fortnight. I stepped away from the desk for a couple of weeks and spent part of it in Aurangabad, where Kohi Cafe poured a few genuinely interesting cups — the kind of detour that reminds you why this directory exists. Back now, and Issue 7 has plenty to catch up on.

The catalogue had a busy stretch: 28 new coffees landed, pushing ICB past 1,120 coffees across 87 roasters. The pattern worth noticing is what kind — anaerobics, co-fermented naturals, and spirit-cask lots filled most of the additions.

Two of the three new roasters are out of Surat, Gujarat — a reminder that India's specialty map keeps spreading beyond the Karnataka belt. Below: a co-fermented natural from Ratnagiri, two new roasters, and three field-guide pieces on how single-origin took over.

Coffee Spotlight.

Ratnagiri Peach Co-Fermented Naturals

Rossette Coffee

Natural · Co-Fermented  ·  Light Roast  ·  Ratnagiri, Maharashtra  ·  SCA 88

Newly listed on ICB, this is a co-fermented natural from Ratnagiri in Maharashtra — an emerging origin well outside the traditional Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu belt. The peach co-ferment and light roast point to a fruit-forward, expressive cup, and the SCA 88 cupping score gives it an objective marker. It arrives with a sibling strawberry co-ferment from the same roaster, part of a fortnight that leaned heavily on fermentation-driven processing.

Just Joined ICB

Roaster Spotlights.

Two newcomers just joined ICB — one single-estate roaster from Karnataka's coffee birthplace, one instant-coffee brand from Gujarat.

Yagachi Coffee

Bengaluru, Karnataka

A Bengaluru brand sourcing single-estate from Blue Mountain Estate in the Baba Budan Giri range of Chikmagalur — the hills where coffee was first planted in India. Its range spans four signature blends from light to dark, each matched to a brew method.

Oishii Reserve

Surat, Gujarat

A Surat brand making specialty-grade instant coffee from Indian estates like Attikan, Baarbara, and Orchadale. Single-origin and experimental profiles — 70% Dark, Fruity Bloom, Shadow Nectar — in a no-equipment format with no fillers or added flavour.
