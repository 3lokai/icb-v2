Welcome to Fortnight 9. Someone on r/IndiaCoffee spent the week telling roasters to quit selling 200g bags at ₹900 and up, and a hundred comments later the sub still could not agree on whether that was fair. Half the replies said they had switched to green tea over prices; the other half said they had been paying ₹1,000 to ₹1,400 a kilo for years without a second thought.

So we ran the numbers across every active listing on the directory, then ran them again on coffees sold in more than one size. The pack size matters less than it looks, and what is inside the small bags matters more.

Also in this issue: 127 new lots, a wishlist you asked for, and 25 roasters quietly settling an argument about monsooned Malabar. Hit reply any time.

From the Catalog

What the bag is worth.

The median 200g bag on ICB works out to ₹438 per 100g. At 500g and 1kg it is ₹240. Which reads as a brutal penalty for buying small — except those rows are five different sets of coffees.

Pack Listings ₹ / 100g
100g 111 ₹500
200g 146 ₹438
250g 921 ₹280
500g 355 ₹240
1kg 350 ₹240

Run it again on coffees sold in more than one size, where the only thing changing is the bag, and the discount shrinks hard: 6% going from 250g to 500g, 13% from 250g to a kilo, 15% from 200g to 500g. The bag is worth a tenth, not a half.

What separates ₹438 from ₹240 is what goes in it. Coffees that stop at 250g run ₹344 per 100g and are 67% single origin; coffees you can buy by the kilo run ₹235, and 47%. Small bags hold the boutique single-estate lots. Kilos hold the house blends. So ₹900 for 200g — ₹450 per 100g, against a 200g median of ₹438 — is the going rate for that tier of coffee, not a surcharge for the size.

Medians across active listings, one price per coffee per pack size, subscriptions and bundles excluded. A snapshot, not a trend.

Daily Driver

The one in the argument.

The two bags the thread was actually arguing about, side by side.

Devan's Monsooned Malabar AA

New Delhi · Medium roast · Arabica AA

₹220  / 100g
₹550 / 250g  ·  ₹1,100 / 500g

The cheaper cup, and the flatter deal: ₹220 per 100g whether you buy 250g or 500g, so there is nothing to gain by sizing up. Devan's has sold coffee in Delhi since 1962, when D. Vasudevan left the Coffee Board of India. One rating on ICB so far, at four stars.

Rate Devan’s →

Blue Tokai Monsoon Malabar AA

Hoysala Estate · Medium-dark · Single origin

₹280  / 100g
₹700 / 250g  ·  ₹2,380 / 1kg

The one that started the thread, and a rare case where the bag really does pay: ₹280 per 100g at 250g falls to ₹238 by the kilo, a 15% cut. Roasted darker than the Devan’s, single estate rather than a graded lot. No ratings on ICB yet.

Rate Blue Tokai →

Both are monsooned arabica; both are medium or darker; neither is the other's substitute. And they are 2 of 30 monsooned lots on ICB, from 25 roasters.

Bloom Coffee Roasters

Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh

Bloom added 20 new lots this fortnight, more than any other roaster on the directory. They work out of Bhopal, a long way from the southern growing belt, and run wholesale-first: 150-plus outlets supplied, with barista training alongside the beans. Small batches, lighter profiles, and an unusual willingness to put serious effort into robusta rather than treating it as filler.
