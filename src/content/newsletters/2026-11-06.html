Coffee Spotlight.

Nagaland Rice Yeast Natural

Bermito Coffee Roasters · Kozhikode · Tsiese Coffee Farm, Nagaland

Arabica Anaerobic Light

Newly listed on ICB, this lot ferments anaerobically with a locally sourced rice yeast culture, timed by sensory evaluation rather than the clock. It then dries on raised beds for about 25 days.

Just Joined ICB

Roaster Spotlight.

Ko Ho Coffee Co

Vadodara, Gujarat · coffees being added

Just joined ICB, Ko Ho Coffee Co is a home-based micro roastery in Vadodara, run by an electrical engineer who moved from home brewing to roasting on a 500g electric roaster. The name loosely translates from Japanese as "bubble of blissfulness". Its small range centres on single-origin Indian naturals, including lots from Koraput, Kodagu and the Pulney Hills.

India trims coffee output outlook after weak monsoon hits crops
