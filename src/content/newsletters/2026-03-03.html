Freshly Added

What's New on ICB.

Nine roasters joined the directory this fortnight, with representation from Meghalaya, Odisha, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Haryana — from a third-generation heritage roastery in Pune to a Northeast India farm-linked venture in Shillong. The 153 new coffees include washed (15), natural (13), monsooned (3), experimental (9), and more — bringing the catalogue to 856 active coffees across 69 roasters. Community ratings reached 140 in total, with 51 added this fortnight.

Just Joined ICB

Roaster Spotlight.

Caramelly
Gurgaon, Haryana

Caramelly, based in Gurgaon, has just joined ICB with 9 coffees currently listed on their profile. Unlike a standalone roastery, Caramelly operates as a broader coffee marketplace — Nespresso-compatible capsules, their own roasted beans, and a curated selection from Indian and international roasters, alongside brewing equipment and accessories. It's a single-destination format designed for home brewers who want to compare formats, machines, and origins without juggling multiple tabs.
