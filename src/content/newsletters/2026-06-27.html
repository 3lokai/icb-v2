Welcome to Issue 8. If you've bought a bag lately, you may have felt it. Coffee is getting pricier, and the squeeze is reshaping what lands on the shelf. Green-coffee costs are at a multi-decade high, and Indian roasters are responding in two ways: bigger price tags, and in a few cases, smaller bags.

We dug into it. Blue Tokai says its green-coffee cost rose 49% year-on-year. Coffeeverse now prices some premium fermented lots at 200g, while the big roasters held 250g and raised the price instead. It's not "shrinkflation everywhere." It's two answers to the same problem, and we've laid both out below.

Also this fortnight: three new roasters (two more from Gujarat), nine new coffees leaning experimental, and a fresh field-guide piece on what actually drives the price of a bag of Indian specialty.
