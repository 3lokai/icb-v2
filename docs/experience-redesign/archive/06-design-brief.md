# 06 — Penpot and Interactive Design Brief

## Tool roles
- **Penpot:** editable scene compositions, navigation states, content presentation, design tokens (later), transition storyboards, annotated prototypes. Connect through its official MCP; first inspect, then edit.
- **Codex/Claude:** inspect existing site, research current experience, translate approved specs into Penpot design work, then implement *isolated* browser prototypes after the UX direction is selected.
- **Browser prototype:** the actual test of scrolling, Three.js, camera, timing and interactive feel. Penpot cannot prove these motion qualities alone.

## What to do FIRST
1. Read all `docs/experience-redesign/*.md` plus existing `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and relevant ADRs.
2. Audit current pages and UI interactions, listing what already works, what lacks cohesion, and technical/SEO constraints. Do not implement.
3. Present two DIFFERENT experience screenplays, each with newcomer, expert and SEO deep-link entry; describe emotional flow and what changes across moments.
4. Make 5–7 annotated linked keyframes per screenplay in Penpot. At least one must illustrate live technical faceting and cross-object serendipity.
5. Include responsive/mobile and reduced-motion states. Avoid turning scenes into moodboards or conventional stacked rectangles.
6. Stop for review and decision BEFORE creating a component library or full site screens.

## Design success criteria
- The whole feels like one experience: composition, typography, visual rhythm, motion, interaction and contents collaborate.
- Visitor sees a clear invitation, available search, and a direct technical path immediately.
- Expert can quickly filter by method/process/roast/origin/price and keep the selection across transitions.
- Coffee knowledge is revealed with warmth and intellectual care without hiding important fields.
- Serendipitous editorial connections are grounded in actual relationships, not random 'related content'.
- Both initial and deep-linked visitors are served; motion is optional and never blocks comprehension.
- Current brand is treated as a constraint to explore rather than discarded by default.

## Deliverables at review
Penpot file link(s), exports of keyframes, written screenplay, matrix of requirements-to-moments, interaction annotations (trigger/state/result/fallback), mobile equivalent, design risks, a short trade-off analysis and the agent's recommendation with confidence and unknowns.
